Complaint Management System

Babu Banarasi Das Group of Institutions

About the System

The Complaint Management System is designed to provide students with a transparent, efficient, and user-friendly platform to communicate their concerns, queries, and feedback. It ensures that student voices are heard and addressed promptly by the administration.

Key Features

1. Complaint Submission

Students can easily submit complaints related to:

  • Academic issues (faculty, exams, syllabus)
  • Infrastructure (classrooms, labs, hostels)
  • Administrative services
  • Other campus-related concerns

2. Feedback System

Students can share feedback on:

  • Faculty performance
  • Courses and curriculum
  • Campus facilities
  • Events and activities

Conclusion

This system acts as a bridge between students and administration at Babu Banarasi Das Group of Institutions, promoting transparency, accountability, and continuous improvement in the institution.
